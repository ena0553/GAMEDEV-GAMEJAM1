**Game Description**

As the PAC-MAN series of organisms continues to grow stronger and stronger against G.H.O.S.T. entities, there is a need for training date.... that's where you come in! Starting from the center of the maze, escape the latest PAC-MAN model as it hunts you down. The model is capable of pathfinding to you, and can teleport at random to any of the 8 pellets scattered along the maze. Audio-visual cues will inform you when he's near. Find your way to any of the 4 exits to escape the same fate as other "training data" before you.

**Controls**
- W - Forward
- A - Left
- S - Backward
- D - Right
- Space - Jump
